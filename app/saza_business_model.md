# 🏢 SalonHub SaaS Business Model

## 💰 Subscription Plans & Pricing

### 📊 Pricing Tiers (Kenyan Market)

#### **Basic Plan - KES 5,000/month**
- **Target**: Small salons (1-5 staff, single location)
- **Features**:
  - Appointment Booking
  - Basic Inventory Management  
  - Staff Management (up to 5)
  - Client Management (up to 500)
  - Basic Reports
  - Email Support

#### **Professional Plan - KES 15,000/month**
- **Target**: Medium salons (6-20 staff, up to 3 branches)
- **Features**:
  - Everything in Basic
  - Multi-Branch Management (up to 3)
  - Advanced Analytics Dashboard
  - Commission Tracking
  - Product Management
  - Financial Reports
  - SMS Notifications
  - Priority Support

#### **Enterprise Plan - KES 40,000/month**
- **Target**: Large salon chains (20+ staff, unlimited branches)
- **Features**:
  - Everything in Professional
  - Unlimited Branches
  - Custom Reports
  - API Access
  - White Label Options
  - Dedicated Support
  - Advanced Security
  - Custom Integrations
