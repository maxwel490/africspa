How Groups Work

Backbar groups allow you to organize staff deductions based on:

  • Specialties: Hair braiders, barbers, nail techs, etc.
  • Employment Type: Full-time, part-time, commission, etc.
  • Custom Criteria: Any combination you need

When you create a deduction in the backbar system, you can assign it to a specific group, and it will only apply to staff members who match that group's criteria.
