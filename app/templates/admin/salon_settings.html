Help & Information
About Internal Shop

The internal shop feature allows your salon to operate a retail shop within the same system, keeping shop inventory and sales separate from salon operations.

About Backbar Deductions

Backbar deductions automatically charge salonists for products used during services, helping maintain accurate inventory and cost management.

Backbar Groups organize deductions by team specialties (e.g., "Cornrows & Braids", "Nails & Makeup"). Each group can have different deduction rules and target specific workers.

About Payment Methods

Configure which payment methods are accepted at your salon. These options will appear in service order forms for accountants to use when processing payments.
