# Beautyspace System User Guide

Welcome to Beautyspace! This guide will help you understand how to use the system as an end user. The system is a web-based platform for managing salon operations, including appointments, staff, inventory, and more.

## 1. Getting Started
- Open your web browser and navigate to the Beautyspace login page (ask your admin for the URL if unsure).
- Enter your username and password to log in.

## 2. User Roles
- **Admin:** Full access to all features, including branch management, staff, inventory, finance, and reporting. See the Admin section below for details.
- **Accountant:** Manages branch finances, inventory, staff, commissions, settlements, and more. See the Accountant section below for details.
- **Salonist:** Handles daily work, appointments, and tracks personal commissions and deductions. See the Salonist section below for details.
- **Other Roles:** May be configured by your system administrator.

## 3. Logging In
- Go to the login page.
- Enter your credentials.
- If you forget your password, use the 'Forgot Password' link to reset it.

## 4. Main Features
### Dashboard
- View a summary of key metrics (appointments, revenue, staff activity).

### Appointments
- Book new appointments for clients.
- View, edit, or cancel existing appointments.

### Staff Management
- Add, edit, or remove staff members (admin only).
- Assign roles and manage permissions.

### Inventory
- Track product stock levels.
- Add new products and update quantities.

### Reports
- Generate financial, appointment, and staff performance reports.

## 5. Navigation Tips
- Use the sidebar or top navigation menu to access different sections.
- Each user role sees only the features relevant to their permissions.
- Click your profile icon to access account settings or log out.

## Admin Role: Features & Workflows

Admins have full control over all branches and system-wide settings. Their main features include:

### 1. Admin Dashboard
- View global and branch-specific statistics: appointments, revenue, staff, and pending invoices.
- Analyze revenue trends over time for each branch.

### 2. Branch Management
- Add, edit, or deactivate branches.
- Assign staff and accountants to branches.

### 3. Staff Management
- Create, edit, or delete workers (salonists, accountants, other staff) for any branch.
- Set roles, salaries, and detailed profiles.
- Prevent deletion of own admin account for safety.

### 4. Finance & Overheads
- Access detailed finance reports for each branch (revenue, commissions, overheads, salaries, profit).
- Record and manage overhead expenses for branches.

### 5. Inventory Oversight
- View and audit inventory and product transactions across all branches.
- Oversee supplier invoices and restocking.

### 6. Reports & Reconciliation
- Generate and review monthly reconciliation and profit/loss reports.
- Track model expenses, commissions, and department adjustments.

---

## Accountant Role: Features & Workflows

Accountants manage the finances and operations of their assigned branch. Their main features include:

### 1. Accountant Dashboard
- View recent appointments, today’s revenue, and low-stock alerts for your branch.
- Search appointments by receipt number or staff name.

### 2. Recording Work
- Log new appointments, services, and sales with receipt, client, staff, and payment details.
- Assign commissions, record tips, and split among staff.

### 3. Inventory Management
- Add, edit, or delete products for your branch.
- Issue products to staff or departments and track deductions.
- View recent inventory transactions and logs.

### 4. Staff Management
- Add, edit, or remove salonists for your branch.
- Assign specialties, reset passwords, and track staff activity.

### 5. Commissions & Deductions
- Calculate weekly payouts for staff, with automatic deductions for inventory, purchases, and back-bar.
- View detailed breakdowns by specialty and staff.

### 6. Back Bar & Deductions
- Add mandatory and individual staff deductions for product usage, damage, or purchases.
- Deductions are distributed in weekly/monthly payouts.

### 7. Settlements & Supplier Invoices
- Record supplier invoices, restock products, and mark invoices as paid.

### 8. Shop Sales
- Record direct product sales to clients and update stock.

### 9. Service Rectification
- Manage and log service redo/rectification cases, tracking costs and staff involved.

## Salonist Role: Features & Workflows

Salonists focus on daily work, appointments, and tracking their own earnings. Their main features include:

### 1. Salonist Dashboard
- View your recent work, appointments, and weekly summaries.
- See your total commissions, tips, deductions, and net payout for the week.

### 2. Work History
- Browse your work history week-by-week.
- View details of each appointment, including services performed, commissions, and tips earned.

### 3. Deductions & Payouts
- See all deductions applied to your weekly payout (inventory, purchases, mandatory back-bar, etc.).
- Net payout is automatically calculated and displayed.

### 4. Profile & Activity
- Update your profile details (where permitted).
- Track your activity and ensure you remain active to avoid deactivation.
